Operation

Local anaesthetic systemic toxicity. Arteries in the head and neck run straight to the brain: 0.5 ml or less injected into one can cause a seizure. Aspirate, inject slowly, and keep the total within the maximum, especially with bilateral blocks and topical lidocaine.
