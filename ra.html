Bipolar hip hemiarthroplasty · THR

PENG with an LFCN block

PENG covers the anterior hip capsule and is motor sparing, but it does not cover the incision. The LFCN block covers the lateral incision.

  • PENG beneath the psoas tendon20 mL
  • LFCN in the sartorius and TFL tunnel5 mL
  • Ropivacaine 0.2%25 mL · 50 mg

Total knee replacement

Adductor canal, three targets

One lateral skin entry, three tip positions from deep to superficial. The saphenous nerve and the nerve to vastus medialis cover the joint; the anterior femoral cutaneous nerves cover the anterior incision.

  • Saphenous nerve beside the artery, deep to sartorius10 mL
  • Nerve to vastus medialis between sartorius and vastus medialis10 mL
  • Anterior femoral cutaneous nerves beneath fascia lata, superficial to sartorius5 mL
  • Ropivacaine 0.2%25 mL · 50 mg
