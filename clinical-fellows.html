Clinical Fellowship in General Anaesthesia

For Clinical Fellows

One year to develop your regional anaesthesia and perioperative practice.

Explore your pathway ↓
The programme

General anaesthesia.
A regional focus.

A full-time clinical fellowship for locally and internationally trained anaesthesiologists who have completed specialist training. Build on your general anaesthesia experience, with a particular focus on regional anaesthesia, pain management and perioperative care.

Train through supervised clinical practice, academic activity and a varied surgical caseload at Ng Teng Fong General Hospital, part of NUHS.

Duration
One year, fixed and non-extendable
Programme Director
Dr Lean Lyn Li
Location
Ng Teng Fong General Hospital

Singapore 609606
01 / Clinical practice

Develop your technique.

Gain supervised experience in ultrasound-guided peripheral nerve blocks, continuous catheter techniques for postoperative analgesia and neuraxial procedures. Extend your approach to acute and chronic pain and complex perioperative care.

02 / Faculty support

Learn with experienced colleagues.

Work with faculty in general and regional anaesthesia and pain medicine. Discuss clinical decisions, review outcomes and develop a safe, sustainable regional anaesthesia practice.

03 / Academic practice

Contribute beyond the list.

Take part in quality improvement, multidisciplinary care and teaching for junior doctors and medical students. Discuss research interests and opportunities with your supervisors.

04 / Entry requirements

Build on your training.

  • Completed specialist training or residency in anaesthesiology.
  • More than 3 years of clinical experience in anaesthesia.
  • A primary training interest in regional anaesthesia or perioperative medicine.
