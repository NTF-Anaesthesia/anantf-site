Which pathway suits you?

Seven short questions about your experience, interests and working style. Answers are not saved or sent.

Programme and entry requirements

A full-time, fixed, non-extendable one-year fellowship, combining general anaesthesia with regional anaesthesia, pain and perioperative care. Learn with experienced faculty through supervised practice, teaching and quality improvement.

  • Completed specialist training or residency in anaesthesiology.
  • More than 3 years of clinical experience in anaesthesia.
  • A primary training interest in general anaesthesia, regional anaesthesia or perioperative medicine.

Discuss application details with the department or the NUHS Graduate Medical Education office.

How the quiz works

A required focus in cardiac, obstetric, paediatric or transplant anaesthesia does not match our clinical scope. Fellowship enquiries require more than 3 years of experience and an interest in general anaesthesia, regional anaesthesia or perioperative medicine.

The three working-style questions contribute up to 9 points. A score of 7 or more suggests a strong fit; lower scores invite a discussion. Duration determines whether we discuss the one-year fellowship, longer-term roles or both.

This is a conversation starter, not a hiring decision. Applications remain subject to completed specialist training, review, registration and appointment requirements.
