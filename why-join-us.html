Working at NTF

Why join us?

Broad clinical work. Support to grow. A team you can speak to.

Find your fit ↘
01 / Work

Build clinical range.

Our caseload spans emergency and trauma surgery, neuro, thoracic, vascular, HPB, upper GI, bariatric and endocrine surgery. Regional anaesthesia, airway management and perioperative care are central interests.

Develop your judgement with senior support, and help juniors develop theirs.

02 / Growth

Make room to learn.

Explore advanced regional techniques, POCUS and airway skills. Bring your interests in ambulatory care, medical education, informatics or improving perioperative workflows.

Learn through mentorship, feedback, case discussions and simulation. Contribute to teaching, exam preparation, audit, quality improvement and research.

03 / People

Good colleagues matter.

We value approachable seniors, respectful teamwork and speaking up. Good relationships with surgeons, nurses and anaesthesia colleagues make a difference to everyday care.

We look for people who take feedback well, support the team and want to make things better. Bullying has no place here.

04 / Lifestyle

Plan beyond the list.

Weekly rostering, fair leave planning and a balanced spread of late lists support a sustainable working life.

Discuss the current rota, supervision and opportunities for your role with us. The right arrangement depends on your career stage and the department’s needs.

Clinical focus

A broad practice, with a clear scope.

If cardiac, obstetric (OBAN), paediatric or transplant anaesthesia needs to be a major part of your next role, NTF will not be the right match for that requirement.

For MOPEX, resident physicians, residents and consultants, we welcome curiosity about regional anaesthesia, perioperative care, airway, POCUS and medical education.

How the quiz works

This is a conversation starter, not a hiring decision or a validated assessment. Your career stage does not affect the score.

The four working-style questions contribute up to 12 points. Learning interests add up to 3 points, one for each selected area. A score of 12 or more, including at least 9 working-style points and one learning interest, gives the strong-fit result. Other scores invite a chat. Any required focus in the four specialties above overrides the score.
