Each clip was cropped to the ultrasound sector to remove scanner headers, names, record numbers, dates, times and locations, then re-encoded with all metadata removed. Per-file processing and SHA-256 hashes are in media-attributions.json.

One source clip showed a patient name, record number, study IDs, hospital text and the scan date and time; these were cropped and masked, and the first, middle and last frames re-checked. Scanner headers with times and an institution preset were cropped from others, the radiograph’s identifiers were blacked out and all file metadata was removed. The slide-master background with logos was not used.
