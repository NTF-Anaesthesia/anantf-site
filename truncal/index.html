Epidural or plane block?

Points that apply to every operation above.

  • An epidural is dense, covers both sides and treats visceral as well as wound pain. Plane blocks treat wound (somatic) pain only, so always add multimodal analgesia. Chin 2017
  • The cost of an epidural: hypotension from the sympathetic block, closer monitoring after surgery, and it is ruled out by abnormal clotting or sepsis, which are common in emergencies. A plane block is often still possible. NotesWilliams 2020
  • Anticoagulants: a paravertebral is a deep block, so follow the same stopping times as for an epidural. Superficial plane blocks at a site you can compress carry less bleeding risk, but still check the drugs and clotting. Notes
  • Single shot or catheter: a single shot lasts hours. When the pain lasts days (rib fractures, thoracotomy, open laparotomy, open liver resection), use a catheter. PROSPECTWilliams 2020
  • Multimodal for everyone unless contraindicated: paracetamol, an NSAID or COX-2 inhibitor, a single dose of dexamethasone where PROSPECT recommends it, and opioids as rescue. PROSPECT

If you choose a thoracic epidural, site it to match the incision. A high thoracic epidural spreads mostly downwards, so site it near the top of the incision; a low thoracic one spreads more upwards, so site it near the middle. Notes
